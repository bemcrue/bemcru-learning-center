The programme

Support that makes learning stick.

Our tuition programme is a structured academic support initiative that complements regular school learning. It targets Junior, Senior, and High School learners. Holiday classes are conducted by highly qualified teachers in manageable groups for individual attention, with regular assessment to track learner progress.

01 / Objective

Improve learner academic performance and mastery of concepts.

02 / Objective

Bridge learning gaps caused by large class sizes in regular schools.

03 / Objective

Build confidence, study discipline, and examination technique.

What makes the programme different

  • We teach learners how to calculate and understand, not just memorise formulas.
  • Regular tests and reports to parents via WhatsApp.
  • Experienced examiners.
  • Adequate learning materials and resources.
  • Digital research centre for learning.

Benefits for learners

  • Improved grades and better understanding of challenging topics.
  • Enhanced problem-solving and calculation skills.
  • Increased confidence in answering national examination questions.
  • Motivation and mentorship for future career paths.
